### SiPS Tips
I have soooo much I want to put here, but I think its better to keep these as long blog posts. 
For now, i'd say that my mindset is pretty much
> You cant rely on discipline/will-power, you need well structured systems and habits to get shit done. 

This viewpoint is inspired by a very popular studying/productivity youtube jvscholz. He used to livestream himself studying like 10-hours a day, anyone would think he is "disciplined" but I think he explains how he does it very well in this [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kICh_d6tHQk)
(jv was very influential to 10th and 11th grade me :sob: good chance I wouldn't have gotten into CMU without him). 

Semi-Quick Tips:
- Read the book atomic habits to sort of formalize how to create systems/habits
- Create "systems" about your certain parts of your life, write it down like a manuel, make it structured(rely on atomic-habit based tips)

- Be ready for your systems to change
