#### Passages

- Frank Barat: [Introduction to _On Palestine_]({{ '/assets/pdf/barat-on-palestine-introduction.pdf' | relative_url }}) (Barat was editor and facilitator of the book by [Chomsky & Pappé](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/books/695-on-palestine)): Centered around the two questions _"How did you become an activist? Why Palestine?"_ and _"How, then, does one become an activist?"_, Barat paints how we are all born activists, and how the world around us turns us away from this innate role in our lives. He speaks on how the modern life almost extinguishes this flame inside us, and how the Palestine crisis reflects all aspects of exactly what's wrong with our world.
